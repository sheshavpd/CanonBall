# Purpose

The refunds service returns money to customers of an online shop when an order did not work out for them. It
decides whether a refund may be paid, sends each payout through the payment gateway, and keeps the money safe
while doing so: no refund is paid twice, and large payouts wait for a person. Customers want their money back
quickly and predictably; support wants rules it can explain; finance wants every payout to be one it can
account for. Every promise in the canon traces back to this page.
