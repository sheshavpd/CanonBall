## Established terms first

A method that invents a new word for every old idea makes its readers learn everything twice, so CanonBall uses the words engineers already know. Where it prefers a plainer word, the established term and its source are given below, so that a reader can connect the method to the literature behind it.

## CanonBall's own names

A few names are the method's own, used where the guide found no single established term for the thing named.

| Name | What it names |
|---|---|
| **Canon** | The set of approved clauses |
| **Clause** | One statement in the canon, of any of the five kinds |
| **Blueprint** | The agents' design for keeping the canon |
| **Verification Record** | The computed status of every clause, shortened to "the Record" after first use |
| **CanonBall** | One release: canon, build and Record together. Also the method. |
| **Verification engineering** | The discipline of building environments in which a clause can be checked |
| **Station** | One of the seven places a milestone passes, from Collect to Operate |
| **Express belt** | The path unplanned work takes: every station, without waiting for a milestone |

## Reading further

For a first reading, Adzic on examples, Leveson on constraints and safety thinking, and Humble and Farley on what a release should be together cover most of the practices the method relies on.
