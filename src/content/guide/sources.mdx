## Why this chapter exists

A method that invents a new word for every old idea makes its readers learn twice. This guide tries to use the words engineers already know. Where it prefers a plainer word, the table below gives the established one, so you can connect what you read here to what you have read elsewhere.

## CanonBall's own names

A few names have no established equivalent, because the thing they name is specific to this method.

| Name | What it names |
|---|---|
| **Canon** | The set of approved clauses |
| **Clause** | One statement in the canon, of any of the five kinds |
| **Blueprint** | The agents' design for keeping the canon |
| **Verification Record** | The computed status of every clause. The guide shortens it to the Verification Record after first use. |
| **CanonBall** | One release: canon, build and Record together. Also the method. |
| **Verification engineering** | The discipline of building environments in which a clause can be checked |

## A note on reading further

If you read only three of these, read Adzic for examples, Leveson for constraints and safety thinking, and Humble and Farley for what a release should be. Between them they cover most of what this guide asks a team to do differently.
