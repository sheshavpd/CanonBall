## The picture

<Conveyor compact />

Read it left to right. Requests arrive as cards. Related cards drop into a tray, and the tray is a **milestone**. The milestone rides the belt through seven **stations**. At the sixth it becomes a ball: a **CanonBall**, which is one release of the system. At the seventh it is running.

## Seven stations

| Station | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| [Collect](../collect/) | Requests gather into a milestone. A one-page proposal says what it will change. | Product manager, elicitor agent |
| [Shape](../shape/) | The promises, decisions and invariants are settled and written down, with the design and the plan for evidence. | Product manager, architect, verification lead, planner agent |
| [Build](../build/) | The code is written. | Builder agent |
| [Verify](../verify/) | The verification environment and the checks are built, and each check is seen to fail before its pass counts. | Verifier agent, verification lead |
| [Review](../review/) | An agent reviews every milestone. A person reviews as well when the change is critical. | Reviewer agent, and the owner of what is at risk |
| [Release](../release/) | The proposed files become the specification, and a CanonBall is published. | A program |
| [Operate](../operate/) | The system runs. What breaks comes back as work. | On-call agent, operator |

Each of the next seven chapters takes one station.

## Two pressures

Work enters the belt from two directions.

**Forward pressure** is people asking for something new: a customer need, a number finance wants to move, an idea.

**Back pressure** is the running system asking for upkeep: an incident, a bug, a reliability problem. A system that is not maintained stops keeping its promises, so this work is not optional and cannot be scheduled.

Both ride the same stations. Planned work rides the main belt as a milestone. Urgent small work rides the **express belt** underneath: it skips the wait for a batch, and none of the stations.

## Three things that are new

A conventional software lifecycle has stages much like these. Three things here are different, and each exists because agents now do the building.

1. **Work moves in milestones, not tickets.** An agent can build a ticket in minutes, so tickets are no longer the unit that needs managing. People's attention is. Gathering requests into a milestone lets people settle its promises, decisions and invariants once, and gives agents a coherent whole to build. [Collect](../collect/) explains how a milestone is sized and when small work may skip the batch.
2. **Verification environments are engineered.** The place where a promise is tested is designed with a person, like any other part of the product. People can trust evidence only if they understand how it is produced. [Verify](../verify/) shows one being extended.
3. **Observability is built for the agents.** Agents build, verify and operate by looking inside the system. Without logs, traces and state they can read, they guess. [Observability](../observability/) says what to build.

## People and agents at the same station

This is not a relay in which people write a specification, hand it over and wait. At each station a person and an agent bring different things to the same piece of work.

| Station | The person brings | The agent brings |
|---|---|---|
| Collect | What matters most, and in what order | Every request, gathered and drafted into one page |
| Shape | The decisions | The questions, the examples and a recommendation for each choice |
| Verify | What would convince them | The environment, the checks and the injected faults |
| Review | Accountability for the risk | A reading of everything that changed |
| Operate | The call on anything risky | The diagnosis |

Nobody waits at the end of the belt. While agents build this milestone, people shape the next one.

## The example

The seven station chapters follow one milestone of a refunds service for an online shop. When it starts, the service is at CanonBall 12 and keeps three clauses:

- **REQ-012.** A customer can request a refund within 30 days of delivery.
- **INV-004.** A refund is never issued twice for the same order.
- **ENV-003.** No payout above $10,000 without review.

The first station is [Collect](../collect/).
