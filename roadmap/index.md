# Roadmap

Milestones in priority order, by cost of delay. The product manager orders the rows; `tools/canonball status`
writes each one's state into its `change.md`.

| Milestone | What changes |
|---|---|

| M3 refunds as store credit | A customer can take a refund as store credit (roadmap/notes/store-credit.md). |
